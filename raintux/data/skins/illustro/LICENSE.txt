illustro (RainTux edition)
==========================

illustro was created by poiru for Rainmeter (https://rainmeter.net) and ships with
Rainmeter as its default skin suite. This folder is an adaptation of it for RainTux.

Changes from the original:
  - Text, comments and links point to RainTux instead of Rainmeter (Welcome skin,
    "Getting Started" and issue links, comments about the tray icon).
  - Disk: Linux mount points (/ and /home) instead of drive letters C: and D:.
  - System: clicking the title opens the desktop's system monitor instead of taskmgr.exe.
  - "Recycle Bin" is now "Trash"; a right-click no longer empties it (Rainmeter asks for
    confirmation first; RainTux shows the skin menu instead).
  - Files are UTF-8 encoded.
